04 Interview prep

Behavioral & case interviews.

The two halves of every consulting interview. Drill cases with the library and platforms above — and practice your story until it’s effortless.

Casing Practice with the DCC case library Case books for live practice — pair up with another member In person Come to a casing workshop Market sizing, profitability, and live practice sessions every semester
Behavioral interviews: structure every story with STAR — situation, task, action, result. Have five stories ready: leadership, conflict, failure, impact, and “why consulting.” Then email the board for a mock behavioral session — we run them throughout recruiting season, and more curated resources are on the way.

The best prep is a partner. Find one at DCC.
