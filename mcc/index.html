What We’re About

What is Management Consulting?

Management consultants provide solutions and strategies to improve the financial and operational health of organizations. Their recommendations are backed by rigorous research, data analysis, and structured problem-solving.

📊
Data Analysis Turning raw data into clear, actionable insights for clients
🧩
Structured Problem Solving Breaking complex business challenges into manageable frameworks
🗣️
Communication & Storytelling Presenting findings that compel clients and stakeholders to act
🤝
Client Management Building trusted relationships and managing expectations
Learn more about management consulting →
Our Purpose
Creating an accessible environment to problem-solve and explore the consulting industry.

The Management Consulting Club’s mission is to expose the next generation of Wake Forest students to the consulting industry and prepare them for career success — regardless of background or prior experience.

We believe the best way to learn consulting is to do consulting. That’s why every program we run is grounded in real-world application.

Accessibility — open to all majors and class years
Real-world application over theory
Peer-to-peer learning and mentorship
Direct connections to top consulting firms
Programs & Activities

What We Do

Six hands-on programs designed to build your consulting skillset from the ground up.

📁

Semester-Long Team Projects

Tackle complex business challenges through multi-month engagements. Conduct research, analyze data, and present strategic recommendations that drive long-term impact for real clients.

🌍

Pro-Bono Consulting

Gain real-world consulting experience by partnering with nonprofits, small businesses, and campus organizations to deliver strategic solutions to pressing challenges.

🏢

Information Sessions & Panels

Learn directly from consultants at McKinsey, Deloitte, Kearney, and more. Ask about a day in the life, company culture, and each firm’s unique recruiting process.

📝

Resume & Cover Letter Workshops

Polish your consulting resume with OPCD staff and our experienced upperclassmen. Highlight your experiences, stand out to firms, and get insightful, personalized feedback.

💼

Case Interview Preparation

Build confidence with the most up-to-date practice strategies. Participate in mock case interviews with alumni and MCC members.

Access our case library →
🏆

Case Competition Teams

Join a team and compete in case competitions across campus. Gain a real consulting experience, deep industry knowledge, and introductions to professionals at top firms.
