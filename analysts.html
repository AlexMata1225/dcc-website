How is the Analyst Program different from general membership?+

General membership is open to all Wake Forest students. The Analyst Program is a selective opportunity within the club, with client-team responsibilities and additional preparation, events, and consultant connections. Analysts remain full members of DCC.

Who can apply?+

The program is open to Wake Forest undergraduates across majors and class years. No prior consulting experience is required.

How does selection work?+

The current process includes a short application and video response, followed by a conversation with members of the executive board. Selected analysts join a pro bono client team.

When is the next application cycle?+

Applications for the fall 2026 cohort are closed. The next cycle will be announced at general meetings and on the DCC Instagram account.

Can I participate while applications are closed?+

Yes. Come to general meetings, attend club workshops, and use the case-prep resources. General club participation is separate from selection into the Analyst Program.
